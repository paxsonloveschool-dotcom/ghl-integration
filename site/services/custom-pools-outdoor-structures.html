How it works

One team, concept to completion

Because we design and build in-house, nothing gets lost in a handoff between a designer and a separate crew.

Consultation

We walk your property and talk through how you want to use it — sun, sightlines, lifestyle, and budget all on the table.

Design plan

You get a customized plan with 2D layouts and 3D models tying together the pool, structures, hardscape, and plantings.

In-house build

Our crew handles site prep, materials, and installation start to finish — with one point of contact the whole way.

Larger pool, kitchen, and structure projects typically run 10–20 weeks. We recommend reaching out 2–4 months before your ideal start date to allow time for design, permitting, and material selection.
